The six pou

Every Part B scenario and mock report is tagged to one pou (NCNZ does not publish fixed exam percentage weights per pou). Standards of competence for registered nurses 2025. How items are reviewed

Every item is original, written from public NZ legislation and standards. Calculation templates are checked by two independent AI reviewers (OpenAI and Anthropic) and then released by the project owner. Published Part B questions have passed the project's publication checks, and high-risk topics also need explicit owner sign-off before publication.

No question has a recorded RN check yet, so none is labelled RN-reviewed. If an RN spot-check is recorded for an exact question version, only that question will say so.
