Heads-up: the exam is in transition (2026–2028)

The Nursing Council is moving Part B questions over to the new pou of the RN standards of competence (effective 1 January 2026). Our questions are written to the new pou, but your sitting may still include items developed under the previous competencies. Use this site alongside your programme’s teaching and the sample questions the Council publishes.

05 / Review

How items are reviewed

Every item is original, written from public NZ legislation and standards; calculation templates pass two independent AI reviews (OpenAI and Anthropic) and high-risk items require explicit owner sign-off. 8 published questions have a recorded RN check for that exact version and are labelled RN-reviewed.
