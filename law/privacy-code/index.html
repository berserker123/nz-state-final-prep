Health Information Privacy Code 2020: nursing guide

The Health Information Privacy Code 2020 sets rules for how health agencies collect, store, use and disclose health information. This page follows the consolidated version in force from 1 May 2026, which includes Rule 3A.

Key provisions

ProvisionPlain-English point中文要点What the nurse does
Rule 1: purpose of collectionHealth information is collected only for a lawful purpose connected with the agency's function, and only if the collection is necessary for that purpose. If the purpose does not need the person's identifying information, the agency may not require it.收集健康信息必须出于与机构职能相关的合法目的，并且为该目的所必需；如果目的不需要身份识别信息，机构不得要求提供。Collect what the care needs, not everything that could be useful.
Rule 2: source of informationInformation is collected from the person concerned, with listed exceptions, for example the person authorises collection from someone else after being made aware of the Rule 3A(1) matters; where the person is unable to give their authority, their representative may be the source of the information (after being made aware of the Rule 3(1) matters) or may authorise collection from someone else (after being made aware of the Rule 3A(1) matters) (Rule 2(2)(b)); or compliance is not reasonably practicable in the particular case.原则上直接向本人收集信息；有列明的例外，例如本人在已被告知 Rule 3A(1) 所列事项后授权向他人收集；本人无法授权时，可由其代表提供信息（在其代表已被告知 Rule 3(1) 所列事项后），或由其代表（在已被告知 Rule 3A(1) 所列事项后）授权向他人收集（Rule 2(2)(b)）；或在具体情况下不合理可行。Ask the person first; use other sources only when an exception applies.
Rule 3: telling the person (direct collection)When collecting from the person or their representative, take reasonable steps to make sure the person concerned (and the representative, if collection is from the representative) is aware of the fact of collection, the purpose, the intended recipients, the name and address of the collecting and holding agencies, whether supply is voluntary or mandatory (and, if mandatory, the particular law), the consequences of not providing it, and the access and correction rights in Rules 6 and 7. This happens before collection or, if not practicable, as soon as practicable afterwards. Exceptions apply under Rule 3(3)-(4) (for example, the steps were taken on a recent previous occasion, or compliance would prejudice the person's interests or is not reasonably practicable).向本人或其代表收集时，须采取合理步骤确保本人（若向其代表收集，则包括该代表）知道：正在收集、目的、预期接收方、收集方和持有方的名称和地址、提供是自愿还是强制（若属强制，须说明所依据的具体法律）、不提供的后果，以及 Rule 6 和 Rule 7 规定的查阅和更正的权利；应在收集前进行，不可行时尽快在收集后进行。Rule 3(3)-(4) 设有例外（例如近期已采取过这些步骤，或履行告知会损害本人利益或不合理可行）。Explain why you are asking and who will see it, in words the person understands.
Rule 3A: information collected from someone elseFrom 1 May 2026, when health information is collected other than from the person concerned or their representative, the agency must take reasonable steps, as soon as reasonably practicable, to make the person (or their representative) aware of the Rule 3A(1) matters (the fact and purpose of collection, the intended recipients, the names and addresses of the collecting and holding agencies, any law authorising or requiring the collection, and the Rule 6 and 7 rights), unless they have already been made aware (Rule 3A(3)) or an exception applies (for example, compliance would prejudice the person's interests, is not reasonably practicable, or would cause a serious threat to public health or safety or to another individual's health or safety; Rule 3A(4)). Rule 3A does not apply to information collected before 1 May 2026 (Rule 3A(5)).自 2026 年 5 月 1 日起，从本人或其代表以外的来源收集健康信息时，机构须采取合理步骤，在合理可行范围内尽快让本人（或其代表）知晓 Rule 3A(1) 列明的事项（收集的事实和目的、预期接收方、收集方和持有方的名称和地址、授权或要求收集的法律，以及 Rule 6 和 Rule 7 规定的权利），除非他们此前已被告知（Rule 3A(3)）或有例外适用（例如履行告知会损害本人利益、不合理可行，或会对公共健康安全或他人的健康安全造成严重威胁；Rule 3A(4)）。Rule 3A 不适用于 2026 年 5 月 1 日之前收集的信息（Rule 3A(5)）。If you receive information about a person from another source, check whether the person has been told, and raise it with your team if not.
Rule 4: manner of collectionInformation is collected by lawful means that are fair and do not intrude to an unreasonable extent on the person's personal affairs.须通过合法、公平的方式收集，且不得不合理地侵扰个人事务。Ask sensitive questions privately and keep them relevant to care.
Rule 5: storage and securityAgencies must protect information with reasonable security safeguards against loss, unauthorised access, use, modification or disclosure, and other misuse, and dispose of documents they will not keep in a way that preserves privacy.机构须采取合理的安全措施防止信息丢失、未经授权的访问、使用、修改或披露及其他滥用，不再保存的文件须以保护隐私的方式处理。Lock screens, do not leave records visible, and use your workplace's secure disposal.
Rule 6: accessA person is entitled to ask an agency to confirm whether it holds health information about them and to give them access. When access is given, they must be told they can ask for correction under Rule 7.个人有权请求机构确认是否持有其健康信息并提供查阅；提供查阅时须告知其可依 Rule 7 请求更正。Do not refuse on the spot: tell the person how to make a request and pass it to the right person.
Rule 7: correctionA person can ask the agency to correct their information. If it is not corrected, the person can provide a statement of correction and ask for it to be attached so it is always read with the information. The agency informs others it disclosed the information to, where reasonably practicable.个人可请求更正其信息；如机构不更正，个人可提供更正声明并请求附于信息之后，使其与原信息一并阅读；机构在合理可行时须告知曾接收该信息的他人。Take the request seriously and route it through the correction process.
Rule 8: accuracy checked before use or disclosureBefore using or disclosing health information, the agency takes reasonable steps to make sure it is accurate, up to date, complete, relevant and not misleading.在使用或披露健康信息之前，机构须采取合理步骤确保其准确、最新、完整、相关且无误导性。Check you have the right person and the current information before acting or passing it on.
Rule 9: retentionInformation is not kept for longer than needed for the purposes it may lawfully be used for, although a document may be kept where that is necessary or desirable for providing health or disability support services to the person.保存信息的时间不得超过合法用途所需，但为向本人提供健康或残障支持服务而有必要或适宜保留的文件除外。Follow the workplace retention and disposal policy; do not keep your own copies.
Rule 10: limits on useInformation obtained for one purpose is not used for another unless the agency believes on reasonable grounds that an exception applies, for example the use is authorised by the person (or by their representative where the person is unable to give their authority), directly related to the original purpose, or necessary to prevent or lessen a serious threat.为某一目的获得的信息不得用于其他目的，除非机构有合理理由相信某项例外适用，例如经本人授权（本人无法授权时由其代表授权）、与原目的直接相关，或为防止或减轻严重威胁所必需。Before using information in a new way, check the purpose it was collected for.
Rule 11(1): limits on disclosureAn agency must not disclose health information unless it believes on reasonable grounds that a listed ground applies, for example the disclosure is to the person (or, where the person is dead or unable to exercise their rights, their representative), authorised by the person (or by their representative where the person is dead or unable to give their authority), one of the purposes for which it was obtained, or information in general terms about presence, location, condition and progress of a hospital patient on the day, not contrary to the express request of the person or their representative.除非机构有合理理由相信某项列明理由适用，否则不得披露健康信息，例如披露给本人（本人已故或无法行使权利时披露给其代表）、经本人授权（本人已故或无法授权时由其代表授权）、属于获取信息的目的之一，或当天以概括方式告知住院患者的在院、所在位置、状况和进展且不违背本人或其代表的明确要求。Confirm who is asking and whether the person has authorised it; if unsure, do not disclose and ask a senior colleague.
Rule 11(2) and (4): exceptionsWhere the agency believes on reasonable grounds that it is not desirable or not practicable to get authorisation, a further list of exceptions applies, including disclosure directly related to a purpose of collection, disclosure by a health practitioner to a person the individual nominated or to a principal caregiver or near relative in line with recognised professional practice and not contrary to the express request of the individual or their representative, or where necessary to prevent or lessen a serious threat to public health or safety or to someone's life or health. Disclosure under subrule (2) is allowed only to the extent necessary for the particular purpose.当机构有合理理由相信获得授权不适宜或不可行时，适用另一组例外，包括与收集目的直接相关的披露、由健康从业者依照公认专业做法向本人指定的人或主要照护者、近亲披露且不违背本人或其代表的明确要求，或为防止或减轻对公共健康安全或某人生命健康的严重威胁所必需；依第 (2) 款披露仅限于特定目的所必需的范围。If you rely on an exception, share only what is needed and record your reasons.
Rule 12: disclosure outside New ZealandWhen relying on the specified Rule 11 grounds, disclosure to a foreign person or entity is allowed only if listed conditions are met, for example the person (or, where the person is dead or unable to exercise their rights, their representative) authorises it after being expressly told the recipient may not give comparable safeguards, or the agency believes on reasonable grounds that the recipient is subject to comparable privacy laws or is required to protect the information in a comparable way. Rule 12(2) is an exception: Rule 12(1) does not apply to a disclosure relying on Rule 11(2)(d) (serious threat) or Rule 11(2)(j) (maintenance of the law or court or tribunal proceedings) where it is not reasonably practicable to comply with it.依据 Rule 11 的特定理由向境外的个人或实体披露时，须满足列明条件，例如本人（本人已故或无法行使权利时为其代表）在被明确告知接收方可能没有可比保护后授权，或机构有合理理由相信接收方受可比隐私法约束或须以可比方式保护信息。Rule 12(2) 设有例外：依据 Rule 11(2)(d)（严重威胁）或 Rule 11(2)(j)（维护法律或法院、裁判庭程序）披露，且遵守 Rule 12(1) 不合理可行时，Rule 12(1) 不适用。Do not send identifiable information overseas without checking with your privacy officer or policy.
Rule 13: unique identifiersAn agency may assign a unique identifier only if it is necessary to carry out one or more of its functions efficiently, must take reasonable steps to make sure identifiers are assigned to people whose identity is clearly established, and must minimise misuse risks (for example by truncating account numbers).机构仅在为高效履行其一项或多项职能所必需时才可分配唯一标识符，须采取合理步骤确保标识符分配给身份已明确核实的人，并将滥用风险降至最低（例如显示截短的账号）。Use identifiers the way your workplace process requires and do not share them more widely than needed.

Scenarios

  1. A nurse wants to photograph a wound on a personal phone to show a colleague. Rule 5 requires reasonable security safeguards against loss and unauthorised access, use or disclosure, and Rule 11 limits who health information may be disclosed to. The nurse does not use the personal phone and asks how the workplace expects images to be captured and stored.
  2. A nurse in a general practice receives a letter containing a person's health information from another provider (not from the person). From 1 May 2026, Rule 3A requires the agency to take reasonable steps to make the person aware of this collection as soon as reasonably practicable, unless an exception or Rule 3A(3) applies. The nurse checks with the practice process whether the person has already been told.
  3. A nurse finds an error in the medication history shown on the screen and the patient asks for it to be corrected. Rule 7 lets the person ask for correction. If the agency will not correct it, the person can provide a statement of correction to be attached. The nurse records the request and passes it through the correction process.

Common mistakes

  • Believing any family member may receive full information: Rule 11 limits disclosure to listed grounds; general information about presence, location, condition and progress is allowed in the terms of Rule 11(1)(e), and nominated-person or caregiver disclosure depends on the conditions in Rule 11(2)(b).
  • Forgetting that Rule 3A applies only to information collected on or after 1 May 2026 (Rule 3A(5)).
  • Thinking a person cannot see their own records: Rule 6 gives an entitlement to confirmation and access, subject to the Act.
  • Treating Rule 10 and Rule 11 as the same: Rule 10 limits using information for a new purpose, Rule 11 limits disclosing it to someone else.
  • Assuming disclosure for a serious threat has no limit: under Rule 11(4), disclosure under subrule (2) is allowed only to the extent necessary for the purpose.
