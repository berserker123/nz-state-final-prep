HDC Code of Rights: NZ nursing quick reference

The Code sets out ten rights for people who use health and disability services, and the duties of providers such as nurses. It is set out in the Schedule to regulations made under s 74(1) of the Health and Disability Commissioner Act 1994.

Key provisions

ProvisionPlain-English point中文要点What the nurse does
Clause 1(1)-(3): rights and dutiesEvery consumer has the rights in the Code and every provider has its duties. A provider must act to tell consumers of their rights and to enable them to use those rights.每位消费者都享有《法典》中的权利，每位服务提供者都承担相应义务；提供者须主动告知并协助消费者行使权利。Be ready to explain a right in plain words and point the person to where they can raise it.
Right 1(1)-(3): respectPeople are treated with respect and their privacy is respected. Services take account of the needs, values and beliefs of different cultural, religious, social and ethnic groups, including Māori.受到尊重，隐私受到尊重；服务须考虑不同文化、宗教、社会和族裔群体（包括毛利人）的需要、价值观和信念。Ask about preferences instead of assuming them; protect privacy during care and conversations.
Right 2: freedom from discrimination, coercion, harassment and exploitationPeople are free from discrimination, coercion, harassment, and sexual, financial or other exploitation.免受歧视、胁迫、骚扰，以及性、财务或其他形式的剥削。Give every person the same standard of care and never pressure someone into a choice.
Right 3: dignity and independenceServices are provided in a way that respects the person's dignity and independence.服务方式须尊重个人的尊严与独立性。Let the person do what they can for themselves and offer choices in how care is given.
Right 4(1)-(5): appropriate standardServices use reasonable care and skill, meet legal, professional and ethical standards, fit the person's needs, minimise harm and optimise quality of life, and providers co-operate to keep care continuous.服务须具备合理的谨慎与技能，符合法律、专业和伦理标准，契合个人需要，尽量减少伤害并优化生活质量，且各提供者之间协作以保证照护连续。Work within your scope and standards, and hand over clearly so care is not interrupted.
Right 5(1)-(2): effective communicationCommunication is in a form, language and manner the person can understand. Where necessary and reasonably practicable this includes a competent interpreter. The environment should allow open, honest communication.沟通的形式、语言和方式须让对方能够理解；在必要且合理可行时包括合格的口译员；环境应便于开放、诚实的沟通。Check understanding, adapt how you communicate, and arrange a competent interpreter when one is needed and practicable.
Right 6(1)-(4): fully informedPeople receive the information a reasonable consumer in their circumstances would expect: their condition, the options with risks, side effects, benefits and costs, timing, any teaching or research involvement, and test and procedure results. They get honest, accurate answers and, on request, a written summary.有权获得处于其情形的合理消费者会期望获得的信息：病情、各选项及其风险、副作用、益处和费用、预计时间、是否参与教学或研究、检查和操作结果；有权得到诚实准确的回答，并可要求书面摘要。Answer honestly; if a question is outside your role, find out who can answer it and tell the person.
Right 7(1)-(3): informed choice and consentServices are provided only with informed choice and informed consent, unless an enactment, the common law or the Code says otherwise. Every consumer is presumed competent unless there are reasonable grounds to believe otherwise. A person with diminished competence keeps the right to decide to the extent appropriate.除非法律、普通法或《法典》另有规定，服务须在知情选择并给予知情同意后提供。除非有合理理由认为其无行为能力，否则推定每位消费者具备能力；能力下降者仍可在适当范围内作出决定。Do not assume incapacity from age, diagnosis or disagreement; involve the person in the decision as far as they are able.
Right 7(6): written consentWhere informed consent to a health care procedure is required, it must be in writing if the person is to take part in research, the procedure is experimental, the person will be under general anaesthetic, or there is a significant risk of adverse effects.当需要就医疗护理操作取得知情同意时，如涉及参与研究、实验性操作、全身麻醉，或存在显著不良后果风险，同意必须以书面形式作出。Check that consent is recorded in the required form before the procedure starts.
Right 7(7): refusing or withdrawingEvery consumer has the right to refuse services and to withdraw consent to services.每位消费者都有权拒绝服务或撤回对服务的同意。Stop and clarify what the person is declining, document it, and tell the responsible team member.
Right 8: supportA person may have one or more support persons of their choice present, except where safety may be compromised or another consumer's rights may be unreasonably infringed.可选择一名或多名支持者在场，除非可能危及安全或不合理地侵害其他消费者的权利。Welcome the support person; if a limit is needed, explain the reason and offer an alternative.
Right 9: teaching and researchThe rights in the Code also apply when a consumer takes part, or is proposed to take part, in teaching or research.消费者参与（或被提议参与）教学或研究时，《法典》中的权利同样适用。Make sure the person knows about and agrees to any student or research involvement.
Right 10(1)-(8): complaintsPeople can complain in any form appropriate to them, to the provider, to a person authorised to receive complaints, or to others including an independent advocate and the Commissioner. Providers must help resolve complaints fairly and simply and keep the person updated at intervals of not more than 1 month. Every provider (unless an employee of a provider) must have a complaints procedure that acknowledges a complaint in writing within 5 working days of receipt unless it has been resolved to the consumer's satisfaction within that period (Right 10(6)(a)). Within 10 working days of that acknowledgement the provider must decide whether it accepts the complaint is justified or, if more time is needed, work out how much and, if that is more than 20 working days, tell the consumer and give reasons (Right 10(7)). After deciding, it tells the consumer the reasons, any actions it proposes and any appeal procedure (Right 10(8)).可以用适合自己的任何形式投诉，向提供者、被授权接收投诉的人或其他人（包括独立倡导者和专员）提出。提供者须公平、简便地协助解决投诉，并每隔不超过 1 个月告知进展；除受雇于提供者的员工外，提供者的投诉程序须在收到投诉后 5 个工作日内书面确认收到（在该期限内已令消费者满意地解决的除外，见 Right 10(6)(a)）。书面确认后 10 个工作日内，提供者须决定是否认为投诉成立；如需更多时间调查，须确定所需时间，若超过 20 个工作日，须告知消费者并说明理由（Right 10(7)）。作出决定后，须尽快告知消费者理由、拟采取的行动以及已有的申诉程序（Right 10(8)）。Listen, do not discourage the complaint, tell the person how to take it further, and pass it on through your workplace process.
Clause 3: provider complianceA provider is not in breach if it took reasonable actions in the circumstances to give effect to the rights and comply with the duties. The provider has to prove that. The circumstances include the consumer's clinical circumstances and the provider's resource constraints.提供者如已在当时情形下采取合理行动落实权利并履行义务，则不构成违反；举证责任在提供者，相关情形包括消费者的临床状况和提供者的资源限制。Document what you did and why, so reasonable actions can be shown.

Scenarios

  1. Mr T, 80, is hard of hearing and is about to be asked to agree to a procedure. The nurse talks quietly at the end of the bed and hands him a form. Right 5(1) is about communication in a form, language and manner he can understand, and Right 7(1) is about informed consent, so the nurse needs to adapt how the information is given (for example, face-to-face, written material, a quiet room) before expecting consent.
  2. A woman wants her daughter to stay during a dressing change. Right 8 gives her the right to a support person of her choice, except where safety may be compromised or another consumer's rights may be unreasonably infringed. The nurse welcomes the daughter and only limits this if one of those exceptions really applies, explaining why.
  3. A patient tells the nurse that a staff member made a rude remark but says he does not want to fill in a form. Right 10(1) lets a consumer complain in any form appropriate to them, and Right 10(3) requires providers to facilitate resolution. The nurse listens, explains where the complaint can go (Right 10(2)), and passes it on through the workplace process.

Common mistakes

  • Treating a signed form as the end of the matter: Right 7(7) says consent can be withdrawn, and refusal is also a right.
  • Assuming a person cannot decide because of age or a diagnosis: Right 7(2) presumes competence unless there are reasonable grounds to believe otherwise, and Right 7(3) keeps decision rights to the extent appropriate.
  • Believing written consent is always required, or never needed: Right 7(6) lists the situations (research, experimental procedures, general anaesthetic, significant risk of adverse effects).
  • Treating the support person right as absolute: Right 8 has two exceptions (safety, another consumer's rights unreasonably infringed).
  • Reading the Code as a guarantee that is breached whenever something goes wrong: clause 3 says no breach where the provider took reasonable actions in the circumstances, and the provider carries the onus.
