Te Tiriti o Waitangi in nursing practice

The 2025 RN standards say registered nurses uphold and enact the principles of te Tiriti o Waitangi, grounded in kawa whakaruruhau and cultural safety. This page links that statement to pou one, the Code of Conduct, the HPCA Act and the current Healthy Futures (Pae Ora) Act.

Key provisions

ProvisionPlain-English point中文要点What the nurse does
RN standards: scope of practice statementRegistered nurses incorporate the knowledge and worldviews of both tangata whenua and tangata tiriti into practice, and uphold and enact the principles of te Tiriti o Waitangi, based on kawa whakaruruhau and cultural safety.注册护士在实践中融合 tangata whenua（本土民族，即 Māori）和 tangata tiriti（依条约而居住于此的人）两方面的知识与世界观，并以 kawa whakaruruhau 和文化安全为基础，维护和践行《怀唐伊条约》的原则。Treat te Tiriti as part of the RN scope itself, not an optional extra: it applies in every setting where you nurse.
RN standards: Te Tiriti o Waitangi and cultural safetyTe Tiriti provides a foundation for working in partnership with Māori to address health inequities. The standards integrate its articles and principles to respect the rights of tangata whenua and help improve health outcomes.《怀唐伊条约》是与 Māori 建立伙伴关系、应对健康不公平的基础。能力标准融入了条约的条款和原则，以尊重 tangata whenua 的权利并帮助改善健康结果。Link what you do to equity and partnership: ask what the person and whānau want, and notice where access or outcomes differ.
Pou one: Māori health, descriptors 1.1–1.41.1 ongoing professional development on Māori health and the relevance of te Tiriti articles and principles; 1.2 advocating for health equity for Māori in all situations; 1.3 understanding social determinants such as colonisation; 1.4 using te reo and incorporating tikanga Māori where appropriate.1.1 持续学习 Māori 健康及条约条款和原则的意义；1.2 在任何情境中为 Māori 争取健康公平；1.3 理解殖民化等社会决定因素对健康的影响；1.4 在适当时使用 te reo 并把 tikanga Māori 融入实践。In an exam scenario, look for the option that advocates, asks and involves, rather than one that assumes or decides for the person.
Kawa whakaruruhau (RN standards glossary)Cultural safety in a Māori context: care that respects and upholds the cultural identity, values and rights of Māori and protects their mana. Grounded in te Tiriti, it protects Māori rights to self-determination and equitable outcomes.Māori 语境下的文化安全：护理要尊重并维护 Māori 的文化身份、价值观和权利，保护其 mana（尊严与地位）。它以《怀唐伊条约》为基础，保护 Māori 的自决权和获得公平健康结果的权利。Cultural safety is judged by the person receiving care, so check with them instead of relying on general rules about a culture.
Code of Conduct (2012): introduction and Principle 2The Code describes the Treaty principles of partnership, protection and participation as integral to nursing services for Māori. Standards 2.5–2.10 include working in partnership with Māori and whānau, understanding Māori health inequalities, recognising that Māori are a diverse population, integrating Māori models of health into care plans, and promoting access to services that meet Māori needs.《行为守则》把条约的伙伴关系、保护和参与三项原则视为向 Māori 提供护理服务的组成部分。标准 2.5–2.10 包括：与 Māori 及其 whānau 合作；了解 Māori 健康不平等；认识到 Māori 是多元群体；在护理计划中融入 Māori 健康模式；促进获得满足 Māori 需要的服务。Use a Māori model of health in assessment and planning when the person wants it, and offer access to Māori health services without assuming.
HPCA Act 2003, s 118(1)(i)The Council's functions include setting standards of cultural competence for nurses, including competencies that enable effective and respectful interaction with Māori.护理委员会的职能之一是为护士制定文化能力标准，其中包括能够与 Māori 进行有效且尊重的互动的能力。This is why cultural competence is part of your registration standards, not only workplace policy.
Healthy Futures (Pae Ora) Act 2022, s 6 (current)As amended on 10 July 2026, s 6 (headed te Tiriti o Waitangi) lists how the Act provides for the Crown's intention to give effect to te Tiriti principles: a Hauora Māori Advisory Committee advising the Minister and Health New Zealand, iwi-Māori partnership boards engaging with local Māori communities, and priorities for hauora Māori in the Government Policy Statement. The separate health sector principles in s 7 were repealed on 10 July 2026.自 2026 年 7 月 10 日起，第 6 条（标题为《怀唐伊条约》）列出了该法如何落实王室践行条约原则的意图：设立 Hauora Māori 咨询委员会，为部长和 Health New Zealand 提供建议；iwi-Māori 伙伴委员会与当地 Māori 社区沟通；政府政策声明须包含 hauora Māori（Māori 健康）的优先事项。原第 7 条的卫生部门原则已于 2026 年 7 月 10 日废除。Do not quote the old s 7 health sector principles as current law. For nursing practice, your direct obligations come from the RN standards and the Code of Conduct.

Scenarios

  1. A Māori patient's whānau ask for time to talk with the patient before he signs a consent form. Pou one descriptor 1.4 and Code of Conduct standard 2.5 point to partnership with the person and whānau. The nurse arranges the time, confirms the patient is still the decision-maker, and lets the team know.
  2. During an admission assessment, a nurse notices the form asks only about physical symptoms. Code of Conduct standard 2.9 asks nurses to integrate Māori models of health into everyday practice and care plans. The nurse asks the patient whether they would like their care plan to cover wairua (spiritual) and whānau needs, and records their answer.
  3. A colleague says all Māori patients prefer the same things, so there is no need to ask. Code of Conduct standard 2.7 recognises that Māori are a diverse population, and the RN standards say cultural safety is determined by the recipient. The nurse asks the patient about their own preferences.

Common mistakes

  • Thinking te Tiriti obligations apply only to Māori health services: the RN standards place them in the RN scope of practice for every setting.
  • Quoting the Pae Ora health sector principles (old s 7) as current law: s 7 was repealed on 10 July 2026.
  • Treating tikanga as a fixed checklist: the standards say to incorporate tikanga where appropriate, and cultural safety is determined by the person receiving care.
  • Assuming the whānau decides for a competent adult: partnership with whānau works alongside the person's own right to decide.
