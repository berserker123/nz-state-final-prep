Admission phrases for nurses

Phrases for welcoming a patient, explaining why you are asking questions, checking language and whānau needs, and making sure the person has understood. Practise saying them warmly and clearly.

Situation

You are a registered nurse admitting a patient to a ward. The person may be anxious, tired or unwell, and may speak English as an additional language. You need to introduce yourself, collect information, explain what will happen, and check that the person understands. This page teaches the language only. What you assess and record on admission follows local policy and the clinical situation.

Phrase cards

English phrase中文When to useNote
Kia ora, welcome. I'm [your name], a registered nurse. I'll be looking after you today.Kia ora，欢迎您。我是 [姓名]，注册护士，今天由我照顾您。First greeting.Introduce yourself and say your role. Use the greeting that suits you and the person.
What name would you like me to use? Am I saying it correctly?您希望我怎么称呼您？我这样念对吗？Checking the patient's name.Ask what name the person would like you to use and how it is pronounced, then use it that way. This is part of culturally appropriate communication (Pou three, descriptor 3.3).
Could you tell me your full name and date of birth, please?请告诉我您的全名和出生日期好吗？Confirming identity.Follow your workplace's identification policy.
What language would you like to speak? Would you like an interpreter?您更愿意说哪种语言？需要口译员吗？Checking language needs.Right 5 of the HDC Code is about effective communication, including a competent interpreter where necessary and reasonably practicable. One Te Whatu Ora district (Waikato) states that it does not endorse family or friends interpreting and recommends authorised interpreters (NZMJ 2026). Follow your service's interpreter policy.
Is there anyone you'd like with you, or anyone you'd like us to contact?您希望有人陪着您吗？或者需要我们联系什么人吗？Asking about whānau and support.Pou three, descriptor 3.4 refers to building shared understanding with people and their whānau. Ask who the person wants involved, and do not assume.
I need to ask you some questions about your health. This helps us look after you safely.我需要问您一些关于健康的问题，这样我们才能安全地照顾您。Explaining why you are asking.Under Rule 3 of the Health Information Privacy Code, take reasonable steps to make sure the person knows why information is being collected, and who it may go to.
The information goes in your health record and is shared with the team caring for you. Do you have any questions about that?这些信息会记入您的健康记录，并与照顾您的团队共享。您对这一点有什么疑问吗？Explaining who will see the information.Keep it short and true. Follow your workplace's privacy policy. Rule 3 covers matters including the purpose, the intended recipients and the person's rights of access and correction.
Do you have any allergies, to medicines, foods, latex or plasters? What happened when you had a reaction?您对药物、食物、乳胶或胶布有没有过敏？当时出现了什么反应？Asking about allergies.Ask about the reaction as well as the name. Record exactly what the person says.
What medicines do you take? Please include things from the pharmacy, vitamins and herbal or traditional remedies.您平时用哪些药？请包括药房买的药、维生素以及草药或传统药物。Asking about current medicines.If the person is not sure what counts as a medicine, ask again in a different way.
Is it all right if I take your temperature, pulse and blood pressure now?我现在给您测体温、脉搏和血压，可以吗？Before an examination or observations.Say what you will do before you do it, and ask for permission. Watch for non-verbal signs that the person is unsure.
This is your bed and this is the call bell. Press it any time you need us.这是您的床，这是呼叫铃。需要我们的时候随时按它。Orientating the patient to the room.Show the bell, the bathroom and the bed controls, and check the patient can reach them.
Would you like to keep your valuables with you or should we help you store them?您的贵重物品想自己保管，还是我们帮您收好？Belongings and valuables.Follow your workplace's policy for recording and storing belongings.
Please tell me if there is anything that is important to you while you are here. For example your beliefs, food, or how you like to be cared for.在这里住院期间，如果有什么对您很重要的事，请告诉我。比如您的信仰、饮食，或您希望被怎样照顾。Asking about cultural and personal needs.This links to cultural safety and whānau-centred care in Pou two and Pou three. Ask openly and record what the person tells you.
I've explained a lot. To check I was clear, can you tell me in your own words what happens next?我说了很多。为了确认我讲清楚了，您能用自己的话告诉我接下来会怎么安排吗？Checking understanding.This is the 'check you were clear' step in the HQSC health literacy guide. If the person is unsure, explain again in a different way.
What questions do you have? Is there anything you'd like me to explain again?您有什么问题吗？有没有什么需要我再解释一遍的？Closing the admission.Right 6 of the HDC Code is the right to be fully informed. Answer what you can and pass on questions you cannot answer.

Model dialogue

  • Nurse: Kia ora Mr Chen, welcome to Ward 3. I'm Sam, a registered nurse, and I'll be looking after you today.

    Kia ora，Chen 先生，欢迎来到 3 病房。我是 Sam，注册护士，今天由我照顾您。

  • Patient: Hello. Thank you.

    你好，谢谢。

  • Nurse: Could you tell me your full name and date of birth, please? And is there a name you'd like me to use?

    请告诉我您的全名和出生日期好吗？另外，您希望我怎么称呼您？

  • Patient: Chen Wei, 4 March 1958. Please call me Mr Chen.

    陈伟，1958 年 3 月 4 日。请叫我 Chen 先生。

  • Nurse: Thank you, Mr Chen. Which language would you like to speak? Would you like an interpreter?

    谢谢，Chen 先生。您更愿意说哪种语言？需要口译员吗？

  • Patient: My English is okay, but for medical words I would like an interpreter.

    我的英语还可以，但遇到医学词汇我希望有口译员。

  • Nurse: Of course. I'll arrange one. I need to ask some questions about your health so we can look after you safely. The information goes in your health record and is shared with the team caring for you. Do you have any questions about that?

    当然，我来安排。我需要问您一些关于健康的问题，这样我们才能安全地照顾您。这些信息会记入您的健康记录，并与照顾您的团队共享。您对这点有什么疑问吗？

  • Patient: No, that's fine.

    没有，可以。

  • Nurse: Is there anyone you would like with you, or anyone you'd like us to contact?

    您希望有人陪着您吗？或者需要我们联系什么人吗？

  • Patient: Please call my daughter. She'll come this evening.

    请联系我女儿，她今天晚上会来。

  • Nurse: I'll note that down. Next, I'll arrange the interpreter, and we'll go through your health questions together once they are here. I'll also let the team know to call your daughter. To check I was clear, can you tell me what will happen next?

    我会记下来。接下来我先安排口译员，等口译员到了，我们再一起过一遍健康方面的问题。我也会请团队联系您女儿。为了确认我讲清楚了，您能告诉我接下来会怎么安排吗？

  • Patient: You will get an interpreter first, then ask me the questions, and you will call my daughter.

    您先安排口译员，然后再问我问题，还会联系我女儿。
