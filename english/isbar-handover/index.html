ISBAR handover and phoning a doctor

Phrases for handing over a patient or phoning a doctor in a clear order: Identify, Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation (ISBAR). Use it to practise saying things out loud.

Situation

You are a registered nurse on a ward. A patient looks worse, or you need to hand over care at the end of a shift, and you must say what matters in a short, clear order. ISBAR is the five-part frame used on this page (the HQSC clinical communication factsheet gives this template and recommends it where a service has no standard tool; that factsheet is written for maternity early warning systems, and it is used here only as a general teaching frame for practising communication): I = Identify (who you are, who you are speaking to, who the patient is), S = Situation (why you are calling), B = Background (relevant history), A = Assessment (what you think is happening), R = Recommendation (what you need). Workplaces may use their own handover formats and local policy comes first.

Phrase cards

Hello, it's [your name], registered nurse on [ward]. Am I speaking to [name and role]?您好，我是 [姓名]，[病房] 的注册护士。请问您是 [姓名和职位] 吗？Opening a phone call or handover (I: Identify).Say who you are first, then confirm who you are speaking to. Name and role, not just first names.
I'm calling about [patient's name], [age], in bed [number].我打电话是关于 [患者姓名]，[年龄]，[床号] 床。Naming the patient (I: Identify).Give the identifiers your workplace requires. Have the chart and latest observations in your hand before you ring.
I'm calling because I'm worried about [patient's name].我打电话是因为我很担心 [患者姓名]。Stating the problem in one sentence (S: Situation).Lead with the concern, then the evidence. Do not start with a long story.
Their respiratory rate has gone up to 28 breaths per minute and their oxygen saturation is 91 percent on room air.他的呼吸频率升到了每分钟 28 次，吸室内空气时血氧饱和度是 91%。Giving current observations (S: Situation).Say the numbers and the unit, in the same order every time. The numbers here are made up for practice.
Their early warning score is 7.他的早期预警评分是 7 分。Giving the total score if your service uses one (S: Situation).Escalation actions for each score band are set by local policy; the HQSC adult chart shows bands such as 6-7 and 8-9.
They came in two days ago after surgery. They have asthma and no known drug allergies.他两天前手术后入院，有哮喘，没有已知的药物过敏。Giving the relevant history (B: Background).Only what helps the doctor decide: reason for admission, key history, allergies. Keep it to a few sentences.
Their observations were normal this morning. The change started about an hour ago.他今天早上的观察指标都正常，大约一小时前开始出现变化。Describing the trend (B: Background).Say what has changed and when. A trend is often more useful than a single number.
I think this may be a chest problem, and I'm concerned they are getting worse.我认为可能是胸部方面的问题，而且我担心他在恶化。Saying what you think is happening (A: Assessment).Give your honest impression in plain words. Say 'I think' if you are not certain; you do not need a diagnosis.
I'd like you to come and review them now.我希望您现在过来看一下他。Asking for what you need (R: Recommendation).Be specific: come now, review within a time, or advise by phone. Do not hint.
Is there anything you would like me to do before you arrive?在您到之前，有什么需要我先做的吗？Closing the call (R: Recommendation).Only do what is within your scope and local policy. Ask if you are unsure.
Can I read that back to you to check I've got it right?我可以把刚才的内容复述一遍，确认我没听错吗？After receiving instructions.Repeating back helps catch mistakes. Follow your workplace's policy on phone and verbal instructions.
I'm still worried even though the numbers look okay. Can you come and see them?虽然数值看起来还可以，但我仍然很担心。您能过来看看他吗？Escalating on clinical concern alone.The HQSC adult vital signs chart says to escalate care for any patient that you, they or their family are worried about, whatever the vital signs or score.
This is urgent. I need someone to come now. Who else can I call?情况紧急，我需要有人马上过来。还有谁我可以联系？No response, or the patient is getting worse quickly.Follow your local escalation pathway and call for help. Stay calm, speak clearly and keep the patient in sight.
Sorry, could you say that again a bit more slowly?不好意思，您能说慢一点再说一遍吗？You did not catch something, in handover or on the phone.Asking is safe and professional. Never guess at a name, number or instruction.
I'll document this and call you again if anything changes.我会把这些记录下来，如果有任何变化再联系您。Ending the call.Record the time, who you spoke to and what was agreed, in a factual and timely way (Pou three, descriptor 3.6).

Model dialogue

  • Nurse: Hello, it's Mere, registered nurse on Ward 5. Am I speaking to the on-call doctor?

    您好，我是 Mere，5 病房的注册护士。请问您是值班医生吗？

  • Doctor: Yes, it's Dr Patel. Go ahead.

    是的，我是 Patel 医生，请说。

  • Nurse: I'm calling about Mr Smith, 68, in bed 12. I'm worried because his respiratory rate is 28 breaths per minute and his oxygen saturation is 91 percent on room air.

    我打电话是关于 Smith 先生，68 岁，12 床。我很担心，他的呼吸频率是每分钟 28 次，吸室内空气时血氧饱和度是 91%。

  • Doctor: What is his early warning score, and what's his background?

    他的早期预警评分是多少？有什么病史？

  • Nurse: His score is 7. He had abdominal surgery two days ago and he has asthma. No known drug allergies. His observations were normal this morning and he changed about an hour ago.

    评分是 7 分。他两天前做了腹部手术，有哮喘，没有已知的药物过敏。今天早上观察指标正常，大约一小时前开始变化。

  • Doctor: What do you think is going on?

    你认为是怎么回事？

  • Nurse: I think it may be a chest problem and I'm concerned he is getting worse. I'd like you to come and review him now.

    我认为可能是胸部问题，而且我担心他在恶化。我希望您现在过来看他。

  • Doctor: I'll come now. Please stay with him and keep his observations going.

    我马上过来。请留在他身边，继续监测他的观察指标。

  • Nurse: Can I read that back? You'll come now, and I'll stay with him and keep monitoring. Thank you. I'll document this call.

    我可以复述一遍吗？您马上过来，我留在他身边继续监测。谢谢您，我会记录这次通话。
